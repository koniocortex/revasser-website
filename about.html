Why rêvasser

To
daydream.

rêvasser is a small-batch gelato brand, trained in Italy and backed by years of culinary experience, a discerning palate and an uncompromising commitment to using the finest ingredients in pursuit of exceptional gelato.

The French word rêvasser means "to daydream." That simple idea has shaped everything we do.

Every flavour begins with an idea—a favourite dessert, a seasonal fruit or a nostalgic classic. We layer our gelato with thoughtfully crafted variegatos and house-made mix-ins, so every spoonful offers a new texture and robust flavour—velvety smooth, crunchy, nutty and sometimes even savoury. It's this interplay of layers that transforms each gelato into a complete dessert experience.

At rêvasser, gelato isn't an accompaniment to dessert—it is the dessert!

Each recipe is thoughtfully composed and carefully balanced, from the base to the final mix-in, creating layers of flavour and texture in every bite. Our flavour combinations are unique, and some pairings have been a revelation for me as well!

Crafted to become the perfect nightcap, rêvasser is an indulgent ritual to end the day.

Meet the Founder

Divija Singh.

I'm a curious traveller—a flâneur—wildlife enthusiast, amateur sitarist, dog lover and occasional writer.

Armed with a law degree, a Master's and a few more diplomas along the way, I chose to follow my passion for the culinary arts. I grew up in a family of avid food lovers and exceptional cooks—perfectionists, really. Food was always my calling.

Over the years, I have worked as a food and restaurant consultant, curating menus, designing dining experiences and creating culinary events across India and abroad.

My love, passion and obsession with gelato eventually took me to Italy, where I trained in Grosseto, Tuscany, in the science, technique and craftsmanship of authentic gelato making.

Today, rêvasser is the culmination of that journey—with one simple endeavour: to create exceptional gelato and make rêvasser synonymous with gelato.

Our Story

The rêvasser
story.

My journey to build rêvasser took me to Italy. From there, I spent months on research and development—refining recipes, testing flavours and perfecting every texture before launching rêvasser.

Every batch is handcrafted in small quantities, using carefully sourced ingredients, honed techniques, precise formulations and an uncompromising attention to detail. The focus has always been on precision, balance and character.

The result is gelato that is elegant, luxurious, nuanced, indulgent and moreish.
