Meet the Founder

Divija
Singh.

I'm a curious traveller—a flâneur—wildlife enthusiast, amateur sitarist, dog lover and an occasional writer. Armed with a law degree, a Master's in Social Welfare & Development, and a few more diplomas along the way, I chose to follow my passion for the culinary arts. I was raised in a family of avid food lovers and exceptional cooks—perfectionists, really. Food was always my calling.

Over the years, I have worked as a food and restaurant consultant, curating menus, designing dining experiences and culinary events across India and abroad. My love, passion and obsession with gelato ultimately took me to Italy. In Grosseto, Tuscany, I trained in the science, technique and craftsmanship of authentic artisanal gelato making. Today, rêvasser is the culmination of that journey, with one endeavour: to create the finest artisanal offering and make rêvasser synonymous with gelato.

Our Story

The rêvasser
story.

After intensive training in Italy, I spent over six months on research and development—refining recipes, testing flavours and perfecting every texture before launching rêvasser.

Every batch is handcrafted in small quantities, using the finest carefully sourced ingredients, honed techniques, formulations and uncompromising attention to detail. The focus has always been on precision, balance and character.

The result is gelato that is elegant, luxurious, nuanced, indulgent, decadent and moreish.
