Why rêvasser

To
daydream.

We are a small-batch gelato brand, trained in Italy, backed by years of culinary experience, a discerning palate, and an uncompromising commitment to using the finest ingredients in pursuit of exceptional gelato. The French word rêvasser means "to daydream." That simple idea has shaped everything we do.

Every flavour begins with an idea—a favourite dessert, a seasonal fruit or a nostalgic classic. We layer our gelato with thoughtfully crafted variegatos and house-made mix-ins, so every spoonful offers a new texture and robust flavour—velvety smooth, crunchy, nutty and sometimes even savoury.
