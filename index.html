Why rêvasser?

rêvasser is a small-batch gelato brand founded on years of culinary experience, a discerning palate and an uncompromising commitment to using the finest ingredients in pursuit of exceptional gelato.

Every flavour begins with an idea—a favourite dessert, a seasonal fruit or a nostalgic classic. We layer our gelato with thoughtfully crafted variegatos and house-made mix-ins, so every spoonful offers complex, contrasting textures and robust flavour—velvety smooth, crunchy, nutty and sometimes even savoury.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions.

Ordering & Payment

How do I place an order?

Simply get in touch with us on 98681 80116, and we'll take it from there.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For deliveries, the minimum order is two tubs. For self-pickup, you can collect even a single tub.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept payments via UPI.

Can I place an order in advance for a particular date?

Yes, you can place an order in advance for a specific date. We usually have stock available, but a little advance notice is always appreciated.

Delivery & Pickup

Do you deliver?

We try to accommodate deliveries within an approximate 10 km radius. For locations beyond our regular delivery radius, we recommend arranging a pickup through third-party services such as WeFast, Uber or Rapido.

All our gelato is carefully packed with ice packs to help keep it frozen during transit.

What are the pickup timings?

Pickup is available daily between 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

How can I arrange a pickup through a third-party service?

You can arrange a collection through WeFast, Uber, Rapido or any other suitable third-party service. We recommend using these services for convenient collection.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery charges are additional and will be charged as per actuals, depending on the location and delivery service.
